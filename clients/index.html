How Organizations Put Capstone Solutions to Work

Banking, Lending & Fintech

AI-Powered eKYC & Liveness Verification for Digital Account Opening

Challenge

Manual identity document checks slow down customer acquisition and expose digital origination channels to photo, video, and synthetic identity spoofing.

Capstone Approach

Deployed deep-learning Facial Recognition and Liveness Detection integrated into web and mobile onboarding workflows, backed by automated KYC verification.

Operational Impact

  • Faster remote onboarding with real-time biometric identity confirmation
  • Stronger protection against presentation attacks and spoofed identities
  • Lower manual review overhead and consistent regulatory audit trails
Enterprise Security & Compliance

Continuous Breach Simulation & Data Privacy Compliance Baselining

Challenge

Annual point-in-time audits leave organizations blind to evolving threats and create gaps in ongoing Data Privacy Law compliance reporting.

Capstone Approach

Combined Unlimited Cymulate Breach and Attack Simulation, penetration testing, ethical hacking, and endpoint compliance baselining and monitoring.

Operational Impact

  • Continuous validation of email, web, endpoint, and network defenses
  • Actionable remediation insights aligned with internal security teams
  • Structured baselining and reporting supporting Data Privacy compliance
Telecom, Insurance & Retail

Omni-Channel Customer Experience & Cloud Contact Center Transformation

Challenge

Disconnected voice, messaging, and CRM tools create long resolution times and inconsistent customer experiences across service teams.

Capstone Approach

Implemented MoEngage Customer Engagement alongside CCaaS / NODA Contact Center and dedicated multi-channel support operations.

Operational Impact

  • Unified customer interaction context across voice and digital channels
  • Faster contact center deployment via Contact Center Quick Start and Seat Hosting
  • Higher service consistency across support, verification, and collections
Healthcare & Multi-Site Enterprises

Integrated Healthcare IT, Managed Service Desk & Core Operations

Challenge

Growing organizations face rising IT complexity across clinical, back-office, and enterprise software systems with fragmented vendor accountability.

Capstone Approach

Delivered a single-source engagement spanning Healthcare IT (medical concierge, EMR, claims), Naumen ITSM / ITSM 365, Managed IT, and Back Office support.

Operational Impact

  • Streamlined IT ticketing and service management visibility
  • Reliable back-office continuity across accounting, HR, payroll, and training
  • Consolidated accountability under one experienced Philippine partner
