Overview

Capstone Solutions Inc. (we, us, or our) operates Capstone Solutions Inc. Corporate Website (https://capstone.ph) and Electronic Notarization Facility (ENF) (https://enotary.capstone.ph) and is the personal information controller responsible for the personal data described in this notice. Our address is 41st Floor, GT Tower International, 6813 Ayala Avenue, corner H.V. Dela Costa St., Bel-Air, Makati City 1209.

This notice explains how we disclose personal data collected through our website. It supplements our Privacy Notice, available at https://capstone.ph/privacy-notice, which explains our collection practices and other processing activities. We handle personal data in accordance with Philippine personal data protection requirements.

2. Service Providers and Independent Recipients

Service providers processing personal data on our instructions are subject to contractual confidentiality, security, restricted-use, and deletion requirements. They are not authorized to use that data for their own unrelated purposes.

Where a recipient independently determines how and why it processes data, it is responsible for its own processing and privacy obligations. Capstone does not share website or service personal data with independent business partners. Disclosures to the Electronic Notary Administrator (ENA) are governed by applicable Supreme Court rules and issuances. We remain responsible for ensuring that our disclosure to any recipient is lawful.

3. Your Choices and Consent

This notice provides information; visiting our website or continuing to use it does not, by itself, constitute consent to data sharing.

Where we rely on consent, we will request it separately and explain the data, recipients, and purposes covered. You may refuse optional sharing or withdraw consent through our Cookie Settings page (https://capstone.ph/cookie-settings), our Contact page (https://capstone.ph/contact), or by contacting us below. Withdrawal does not invalidate processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal. We will stop future processing based solely on the withdrawn consent and notify relevant recipients where required.

Effect of refusing or withdrawing consent: Refusing optional cookies or optional sharing does not affect core website browsing on capstone.ph. However, declining required contact details prevents submitting a consultation inquiry, and declining required session, identity-verification, or notarial records prevents accessing logged-in features on enotary.capstone.ph or completing an electronic notarization.

4. Overseas Transfers and Access

Primary personal data is stored and accessed in the Philippines and Singapore, with encrypted backup storage maintained in Mumbai, India. No remote access outside the Philippines occurs except as strictly necessary for authorized backup and infrastructure administration.

Where overseas processing occurs, we assess the risks and use encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access control (RBAC), attribute-based access control (ABAC), and contractual or other lawful safeguards designed to maintain comparable protection. Details of the safeguards can be requested through our privacy contact, subject to protection of confidential and security-sensitive information.

5. Retention and Protection

We retain personal data used for sharing for two years after the last communication for inquiry and correspondence records; five years after account closure or service end for account, service, and identity-verification records; ten years for transaction, billing, and payment records; and for the statutory and regulatory retention periods required under applicable Philippine notarial rules for notarial registers, instruments, and supporting records. Our approved sharing arrangements require recipients to limit retention to the agreed purposes and periods, unless further retention has an independent lawful justification. Backup deletion occurs within a maximum of 60 days after primary data deletion via automated cryptographic erasure and secure deletion, with RBAC and ABAC access restrictions pending expiry, subject to necessary legal preservation requirements.

We use reasonable organizational, physical, and technical safeguards, including RBAC and ABAC access restrictions, encryption in transit and at rest, secure transfer methods, and contractual protections appropriate to the data and risks. No online transmission or storage system is entirely risk-free. Where a breach requires notification, we will notify the competent privacy regulator and affected individuals within the required period.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to the conditions and exceptions governing each right, you may request information about our processing, access to your personal data, correction, objection, erasure or blocking, and data portability where available. You may also withdraw consent and seek redress for unlawful processing.

Send requests to our privacy contact below. We may request proportionate information to verify your identity and clarify your request. We will respond within the required period and explain any lawful reason for refusing or limiting a request. If a request concerns an independent recipient's processing, we will explain how to contact that recipient and provide assistance where required.

You may lodge a complaint with the Philippine National Privacy Commission at https://privacy.gov.ph/. You do not have to waive your rights or first obtain our permission to complain.
