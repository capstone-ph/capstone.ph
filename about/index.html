About Capstone Solutions Inc.

18+ Years of Empowering Enterprises Through Technology & Process Excellence

Established in 2008, Capstone Solutions Inc. bridges global deep-learning AI innovation, enterprise cybersecurity, and dependable IT-BPM delivery for organizations across the Philippines.

Our Story & Heritage

From Single-Source IT-BPM to Deep-Learning AI Leadership

Capstone Solutions Inc. (Capstone) has been at the forefront of Information Technology and Business Process Management services for over 18 years. Led by experienced industry professionals, the company has continuously evolved its offerings to equip Philippine enterprises with proven, world-class technology.

We are committed to helping organizations realize the value of a single-source IT-BPM solutions and service chain—spanning consulting, solution supply and integration, software development, cybersecurity, infrastructure hosting, and managed business processes.

Driven by the core mindset that partner satisfaction is the only measure of true customer service, our team works to sharpen our partners’ marketplace competitiveness, deliver superior customer experiences, balance costs, and elevate operational efficiency.

Partner Satisfaction First

We measure our success solely through the operational outcomes, security posture, and satisfaction of the organizations we serve.

Practical Innovation

We bring proven global AI and cybersecurity capabilities into production with clear business ROI and manageable cost structures.

Security & Compliance by Design

Information security and Data Privacy compliance are embedded across our consulting, software, and managed service practices.

Service Excellence

Our people continually refine processes, technical skills, and delivery standards to act as a long-term strategic partner.
