Legal & Privacy

Privacy Notice

How Capstone Solutions, Inc. collects, uses, stores, discloses, and protects personal data across capstone.ph and enotary.capstone.ph in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Organization
Capstone Solutions Inc.
Effective Date
November 1, 2026
Last Updated
November 1, 2026
Website or Service
Capstone Solutions Inc. Corporate Website (https://capstone.ph) and Electronic Notarization Facility (ENF) (https://enotary.capstone.ph)

1. About This Notice

Capstone Solutions, Inc. ("Capstone," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and otherwise process personal data in connection with the website or service identified above (the "Service"), and how you may exercise your privacy rights.

This Notice applies to individuals who use the Service and to individuals who sign documents or act as authorized representatives in connection with the Service. Capstone acts as a personal information controller when it determines the purposes and means of processing personal data to operate the Service, manage its relationships with users and their organizations, and respond to inquiries and privacy requests. Where Capstone processes personal data submitted through the Service solely on another controller's instructions, it acts as a personal information processor for that controller; that controller's privacy notice and instructions also govern that processing, and we will direct relevant requests to that controller as appropriate.

We process personal data in accordance with the Republic of the Philippines' Data Privacy Act of 2012, its implementing rules and regulations, and relevant issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

2. Personal Data We Collect and Its Sources

We collect and process the following personal data:

The personal data we collect and process, to the extent applicable to your transaction or interaction with us, includes your name and contact details; information and images from identification documents you provide; transaction and notarial records, including documents submitted for notarization and signatures; and audio or video recordings and technical data generated when you use our services. These records may contain sensitive personal information or privileged information if you provide it to us in connection with the transaction or notarization.

We obtain personal data from: We obtain personal data directly from you when you provide identification documents, contact details, transaction or notarial documents, or other information; from recordings made during your interaction with us; and automatically from the devices and systems you use to access our services. We may also receive personal data from other parties to your transaction or their authorized representatives when they submit documents or information involving you.

The information you must provide, the reason it is required, and the consequences of not providing it are: For a notarial service, you must provide the identification documents and information needed to verify your identity and the transaction or notarial documents needed to perform the requested act. These are legal requirements; if you do not provide them, we cannot perform the notarial act. For other requested services, you must provide the contact details and transaction documents needed to communicate with you and perform the service. These are contractual requirements; if you do not provide them, we may be unable to process your request or provide the affected service. Other information you choose to provide is optional; withholding it will not prevent us from providing a service unless it is needed for that service, in which case we will explain the requirement and the consequence of withholding it before collecting it.

Please do not provide personal data that is unnecessary for the relevant purpose. If you submit another person's data, you must have authority to do so and provide that person with this Notice where required. This does not relieve Capstone of its own transparency and lawful-processing obligations.

3. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

We rely only on a legal basis that is available for the particular data and processing activity. Contractual necessity or legitimate interests alone do not authorize processing sensitive personal information or privileged information. Where we rely on legitimate interests for ordinary personal information, we assess the necessity of the processing and its impact on your rights and freedoms.

Where consent is required, we obtain it separately, in a specific, informed, and recorded form before the relevant processing. This Notice is not itself a request for consent, and using the Service does not by itself constitute consent. You may withdraw consent through the contact details below. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully undertaken before withdrawal or processing supported by another valid legal basis. We will explain any resulting limitations on the Service.

We will provide additional notice and obtain any required consent before using your data for a new purpose that is incompatible with the purpose originally disclosed.

4. How We Process Personal Data

Our processing activities and methods are:

Capstone collects personal data through information you provide and records and stores it in electronic systems or, where applicable, paper records. Authorized personnel may verify it against information you provide or other sources identified in this notice, access it as needed for the stated purposes, and transmit it to recipients identified in this notice using appropriate safeguards. Capstone disposes of personal data when it is no longer required, subject to applicable retention obligations, by securely deleting electronic records or destroying paper records. Processing may be manual or electronic.

Cookies and similar technologies: Before using any non-essential cookies or similar technologies, Capstone will provide a cookie notice identifying the technologies used, the data collected, their purposes, providers, duration, and available controls, and will obtain consent where required. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect site functionality. Capstone will not use non-essential cookies or similar technologies unless the required notice and controls are available. See our Cookie Settings Declaration at https://capstone.ph/cookie-settings.

Automated decision-making or profiling: Capstone will not use personal data for decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects unless it first provides notice of the purpose, relevant logic, significance, likely consequences, and available rights or review procedures, and implements any safeguards required by applicable law. For information about any such processing or to request available human review, contact Capstone using the contact details in this notice.

5. Disclosure and Sharing

We disclose personal data to the Electronic Notary Administrator (ENA) or another officer authorized by the Supreme Court only if the recipient, data, purpose, and legal basis are identified below and the disclosure is necessary for that purpose.

The scope of this disclosure for the Electronic Notarization Facility (ENF) hosted at https://enotary.capstone.ph is:

  • Data disclosed: Notarized electronic documents, notarial register entries, signer identity and verification records, audio-video session records, and related electronic notarial records.
  • Purpose: Compliance with Philippine electronic notarization rules and regulatory supervision.
  • Authority or legal basis: Supreme Court Rules on Electronic Notarization and applicable Philippine notarial regulations.
  • Recipient's role: Independent public and regulatory authority authorized by the Supreme Court of the Philippines to administer and supervise electronic notarization.

Other recipients and service providers: Any other recipients, including service providers that process data on our behalf, are limited to: website hosting and static content delivery providers (GitHub Pages for capstone.ph), contact form delivery providers (FormSubmit.co for consultation inquiries), and authorized technical infrastructure, platform maintenance, and support providers acting solely on Capstone's documented instructions to operate and secure the Service. Capstone does not share personal data with independent business partners.

When we engage a processor, we require appropriate contractual protections, confidentiality, security measures, and processing limited to our documented instructions. When a recipient independently determines its processing purposes and means, its own legal duties and privacy notice apply to that processing. Disclosures in response to a lawful order or binding legal obligation will be limited to what the requirement authorizes or requires.

6. Storage and Transfers Outside the Philippines

Personal data is stored or accessed in the following locations: Primary systems and personal data are stored and accessed in the Philippines and Singapore, with encrypted backup storage maintained in Mumbai, India.

Transfers or remote access outside the Philippines, if any, and the safeguards used are: Primary processing is hosted in the Philippines and Singapore, with encrypted backups stored in Mumbai, India. No remote access outside the Philippines occurs except as strictly necessary for authorized backup and infrastructure administration. All data is protected by encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access control (RBAC), attribute-based access control (ABAC), and contractual data-protection safeguards.

Where Capstone is the personal information controller, we remain accountable for personal data transferred to a third party for processing, whether within or outside the Philippines, and use contractual or other reasonable means to provide a comparable level of protection.

7. Retention and Disposal

We retain personal data for the following periods or according to the following criteria:

We retain inquiry and correspondence records for two years after the last communication; account and service records for five years after the account closes or the service ends; transaction, billing, and payment records for ten years after the relevant transaction or the end of the applicable tax year, whichever is later; and identity-verification records for five years after the verification or, if used for a service, five years after that service ends. We retain notarial registers, instruments, and supporting records for the period required by applicable Philippine notarial rules and any lawful archival or turnover requirements, measured from the date of the notarial act or the close of the relevant register, as applicable; we do not dispose of records required to be turned over to the proper authority. For other personal data, we retain it until the disclosed purpose is completed, then for no more than two years, unless a longer period is required by applicable law or justified under the following paragraph.

We retain data only as long as necessary for its disclosed purpose, to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, for legitimate business purposes consistent with privacy requirements, or for periods required by law. A legal hold may suspend ordinary disposal for the affected records. We do not retain data indefinitely for an unspecified future use.

Backup deletion and disposal procedures: When retention is no longer justified, we securely delete, destroy, or irreversibly anonymize the data. Backups expire and are deleted within a maximum of 60 days after primary data deletion. Pending expiration, backups are subject to strict RBAC and ABAC access restrictions and are disposed of through automated cryptographic erasure and secure deletion.

8. Security and Incident Response

We use reasonable and appropriate organizational, physical, and technical safeguards proportionate to the nature of the data, the risks of processing, and our operations, including encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access control (RBAC), and attribute-based access control (ABAC).

No storage or transmission method is entirely risk-free. We maintain procedures to investigate and respond to personal data breaches and will notify the NPC and affected individuals within the periods required by Philippine privacy rules when the relevant notification conditions are met.

9. Your Rights

Subject to the conditions and exceptions under Philippine data privacy rules, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about the collection and processing of your personal data.
  • Access your personal data and information about its sources, recipients, processing, and disclosures.
  • Object to processing, including processing for direct marketing, automated processing, or profiling, where the right applies.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Request erasure or blocking where the legal grounds for that remedy are met.
  • Data portability, where data is processed electronically in a structured and commonly used format on the basis of consent or contract and the relevant conditions are satisfied.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Seek indemnification for damage sustained from inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained, or unauthorized use of personal data, where the legal requirements for recovery are met.
  • Lodge a complaint with the NPC.

These rights do not necessarily require deletion or cessation of processing where a valid statutory retention duty or another legally recognized exception applies. For example, withdrawing consent does not require us to destroy records that must be retained under binding notarial recordkeeping requirements. We will explain any restriction or refusal and its basis.

  • Privacy contact / Data Protection Officer: May Cuevas (may.cuevas@capstone.ph)
  • Capstone office address: 41st Floor, GT Tower International, 6813 Ayala Avenue, corner H.V. Dela Costa St., Bel-Air, Makati City 1209

We will not require more information than reasonably necessary for verification and will respond within the period prescribed for the request under Philippine privacy rules. If we cannot fulfill a request, we will explain the reason and available recourse, except to the extent disclosure is legally prohibited.

You may also contact the National Privacy Commission through its official website, https://privacy.gov.ph/, for current complaint procedures and contact details. Contacting us first does not remove your right to seek assistance from the NPC, subject to its complaint procedures.

11. Changes to This Notice

We may update this Notice to reflect changes in our processing activities or legal obligations. We will publish the updated Notice on the publicly accessible privacy notice page of Capstone's official website and update its effective date. We will keep the current Notice available on that page. We will give appropriate notice of material changes before the changed processing begins and obtain fresh consent where required. An update to this Notice does not itself authorize processing that requires a separate legal basis or consent.
