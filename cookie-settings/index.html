It supplements our Privacy Notice at https://capstone.ph/privacy-notice.

2. What Cookies Do

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, tags, and software identifiers. References to cookies in this Declaration include these technologies where relevant.

Session cookies generally expire when your browsing session ends. Persistent cookies remain until their stated expiry or earlier deletion. First-party cookies are set by our Website; third-party cookies are set by another provider. Depending on their configuration, cookies may process device identifiers, IP addresses, browser information, preferences, and browsing activity. A cookie that does not identify you by name may still involve personal data.

5. Your Choices

Because capstone.ph and enotary.capstone.ph currently deploy only strictly necessary session and authentication cookies for logged-in access and do not deploy optional preference, analytics, or marketing cookies, no non-essential tracking scripts are loaded.

If optional cookie categories are introduced in the future, on your first visit the cookie interface will offer these controls with comparable prominence and ease of use:

  • Accept All Optional Cookies: enable all available optional categories.
  • Reject Optional Cookies: keep optional categories disabled and use only strictly necessary cookies.
  • Customize Settings: choose individual optional categories and save your preferences.

Optional categories will not be preselected. Closing a banner, scrolling, continuing to browse, or accepting Website Terms and Conditions does not constitute consent to optional cookies. You can revisit this Cookie Settings Declaration at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the Website footer.

6. Withdrawal and Browser Controls

Where optional cookies are used, you may withdraw or change optional consent at any time through Cookie Settings. Your change applies to future consent-based collection and processing and does not invalidate earlier lawful processing. We will stop the affected optional technologies and delete or disable associated cookies within our control. Any continued retention or processing requires a separate lawful basis disclosed in our Privacy Notice.

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent sign-in, security checks, and access to logged-in content on enotary.capstone.ph from working. Choices normally apply to the browser and device on which they are made unless the settings interface expressly states otherwise.

7. Third-Party Features and Notarization Services

Where the Website embeds external features, the inventory will identify the associated providers and technologies. Optional third-party features that place nonessential cookies will load only after the relevant consent. External websites you choose to visit operate under their own notices and settings.

Cookie consent does not authorize audiovisual recording, biometric processing, access to a camera or microphone, or use of notarization documents for unrelated purposes. Any required notices, permissions, or express consent for electronic-notarization services are obtained separately. Accepting a recording consent does not activate optional cookies.

8. Preference Records and Changes

Where optional consent records are maintained, we keep a proportionate record of your cookie choice, including the choice made, its date, and the version of the notice presented, for 12 months after the choice is made or last changed, unless a longer period is required by law or reasonably necessary to resolve a dispute. We do not use that record for unrelated tracking.

We will update this Declaration and the inventory when our technologies or purposes change. Before activating a new optional purpose or materially expanding previously authorized processing, we will provide updated information and obtain fresh consent. An update to this Declaration alone does not authorize additional optional tracking.

9. Privacy Rights and Contact

Where cookie information constitutes personal data, Philippine data-protection requirements apply. You may request information, access, correction, objection, erasure or blocking, and portability where the conditions for those rights are met. Some requests may be limited by mandatory retention obligations or another lawful basis; we will explain any limitation.

For questions, withdrawal assistance, or privacy requests, contact Capstone Solutions, Inc. via our Data Protection Officer at info@capstone.ph, at 41st Floor, GT Tower International, 6813 Ayala Avenue, corner H.V. Dela Costa St., Bel-Air, Makati City 1209, or through https://capstone.ph/contact.

You may also raise a privacy complaint with the National Privacy Commission (https://privacy.gov.ph/). This Declaration does not waive or restrict mandatory privacy rights.
