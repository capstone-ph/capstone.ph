Electronic Notarization (Philippines ENF)

We.Signed

Electronic Notarization Facility (ENF) built for the Philippine Supreme Court Rules on Electronic Notarization (A.M. No. 24-10-14-SC).

We.Signed streamlines legally compliant electronic notarization for individuals, enterprises, and Electronic Notaries Public (ENPs)—supporting both In-Person (IEN) and Remote (REN) workflows with integrated identity verification, geolocation controls, and tamper-evident digital notarial records.

Ideal Sectors: Banks, Lending & Fintech, Real Estate, Legal & Corporate Teams, Enterprises

ENF Platform Features

  • Dual-mode support for In-Person Electronic Notarization (IEN) and Remote Electronic Notarization (REN)
  • Multi-layered eKYC identity verification and live synchronous videoconferencing for principals and witnesses
  • Built-in geolocation verification with VPN detection to enforce Philippine territorial and consular rules
  • Tamper-evident electronic notarial seals, QR-code document authentication, and PDF/A support
  • Automated Electronic Notarial Register with real-time logging, time-stamped audit trails, and session recordings
